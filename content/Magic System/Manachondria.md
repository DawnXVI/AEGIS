The microscopic vessels of the body that produce mana. Properly harnessing magic wouldn't be possible without these.

---

## Overview

Manachondria are organelles that exist within the cells of the body. The amount of manachondria in your body (your manachondria count) depends entirely on your race, as each race's biology is different from each other, and all have more or less manachondria than others.

Manachondria play an extremely vital role. They are what allow people to harness magic. The process of mana production is called **Thaumatopoiesis**. The way it works is:

- The body absorbs residual mana from the air
- The manachondria take this residual mana and convert it into usable mana
- The mana is then stored within the body

Manachondria count also plays roles in how strong one's magic is, how strong it can _get,_ how fast mana replenishes, etc.

As cells multiply and develop throughout an individual's natural growth from infancy to adulthood, more manachondria develop as well. This causes mana production speed and mana capacity to increase naturally over time, until they stop at their peak when the individual reaches their prime.

Humans have the highest average manachondria count of any other race, which is the main reason for their magical superiority.

## Residual Mana

Residual Mana is an invisible, intangible substance in the air—a "magical radiation", if you will—that is left behind as a natural byproduct when anyone uses magic in any way. Every spell cast, every flame conjured, every object levitated always leaves behind traces of residual mana. It's the exhaust of the magic engine.

Residual mana is present across the entire planet, and is needed by the body to produce more mana. Without it, mana production grinds to a halt. The **"Mana Cycle"** is a known and studied natural phenomenon that describes the process of how magic usage fuels more magic usage. A spell is cast, residual mana is release, that residual mana is absorbed by the body and the manachondria, the manachondria make more mana, and it starts all over again.

Residual mana is also used by various species of flora to fuel different biological processes. Some plant species will _only_ grow or bloom in residual mana-rich environments, and some species have evolved to be entirely dependent on it. There are many flowers with beautiful, glowing petals as a result of the residual mana coursing through them. These flowers make great decorations, or they can be a gift for that special someone.

Residual mana is present in higher concentrations within cities due to the dense populations, many of whom are using magic every day and keeping the residual mana concentration high. Within more rural areas or the wilderness, residual mana concentration is lower. While residual mana concentration normally doesn't affect mana production speed (since the body can control the rate at which it absorbs residual mana), being in an area where residual mana concentration is _too_ low can cause mana production to stop entirely due to your body having already absorbed what little residual mana was in the air. Obviously, this is easily solved by simply moving somewhere with a higher residual mana concentration in the air.
