Aisha plunges deeper into the cavern, kicking away rock after rock, boulder after boulder. Her ears twitch frantically, pivoting and readjusting, zeroing in on the exact chamber where the screams are coming from. The light from the stone clutched tightly in her hand trails behind her, keeping the path lit for everyone else.

Not far behind, Leilanna and the team follow the light, already struggling to keep pace. A violent tremor shakes the cavern—and their bodies—as Aisha kicks an obstructing boulder to pieces. Stalactites plummet from above, but two royal mages at the back raise their scepters, freezing the debris mid-air with levitation magic.

Leilanna unsheathes her daggers. Magic radiates through the enchanted steel as she leaps high, her slit pupils dilating and locking onto the suspended stalactites. In a display of feline ferocity, she spins through the air, daggers erupting into bright orange flames as their blades cleave through stone like it's nothing.

The force of her slashes sends the fragments scattering in opposite directions, away from the team as they descend deeper. Leilanna's scowl deepens. Aisha's being too reckless.

---

Left. Right. Right. Left. Right. Aisha kicks off the ground relentlessly, making sharp turns, pushing deeper underground. The steel on her boots strikes the moist, gritty terrain hard enough to leave small craters. Ancient stalagmites that have stood for centuries—millennia—shatter from the impact of her feet and the shockwaves as she tears through more stone to widen the passage. The light stone in her hand keeps shining, still leaving a glowing path for the others.

Though at this point, she's gotten far enough that the tremors she's creating barely reach Leilanna and the team.

Her legs are aching now. A dull, persistent pain. This is the first time she's had to use this much strength just to reach someone. She stumbles—just slightly. She's pulled a muscle. It barely slows her down. She doesn't even notice.

_"NO! STAY BACK!"_

The words are followed by a scream of terror. Then pain. They reach Aisha's ears much more clearly now. Every warning her body has been screaming at her gets thrown out the window. She grits her teeth, resolve flaring white-hot.

She's almost there.

---

Hikari's back hits the wall hard, the force of slamming into a rough and rocky surface sending sharp pain through him as he slumps to the ground.

He struggles to push himself up. A terrible stinging burn spreads across his chest, but he isn't sure if it's from deep claw gouges or fractured ribs.

His heart is pounding. He can't think. His instincts are screaming at him to run. To save his own life.

But his legs won't move. He tries desperately to crawl away, but the adrenaline isn't enough to dull the aches wracking every part of his body.

The creature creeps closer, its movements sluggish and weak. Its many red eyes are the only source of light in this chamber, illuminating just a portion of its head. It lets out an unsettling sound—something between a high keen and a raspy groan. One of its arms rises in the darkness, about to land the fatal strike.

_**BOOM!**_

The leftmost wall of the cavern, for lack of a better term, _**explodes.**_ Rock crumbles inward as a blur rushes through the hole and dust.

_0.3 seconds._

The creature begins to turn towards Aisha. Hikari doesn't look at her. He's staring at the creature, the light beaming from the stone in Aisha's hand finally illuminating its body. The sight horrifies Hikari even further. Long, spindly limbs. A head covered in red, glowing eyes. A mouth sporting mandibles and jagged fangs. A segmented body covered in horn-like structures.

_0.4 seconds._

Aisha scans her surroundings. The flat wall to the right. The shallow pools of water in the chamber. A small hole to the left—a path leading deeper into another part of the cavern. The much larger hole behind the creature, the one it emerged from.

_0.55 seconds._

She looks up. Notices the stalactites covering the ceiling. Notices a large, tightly-packed cluster of them just above the creature.

She runs the calculations. Plans her course of action.

Then, the execution.

_0.8 seconds._

She reaches the creature before it can fully turn towards her. Her leg flies up, her shin **slamming** into its underside with the force of a dragon whipping its tail. The creature launches upward before it even registers what hit it. The shockwave reverberates throughout the cavern, brutal enough to knock loose smaller stalactites even outside the chamber.

The creature doesn't die immediately. The half-dozen stalactites above it—each one sharpened to a fine point—punch through its body like spears through paper and burst through the other side, dripping with black ichor.

The creature lets out a horrible screech, its entire body seizing and convulsing in agony before going limp. For a moment, it just _hangs_ there. Then, its weight pulls it downward. It falls. Slams into the ground with a sickening thud.
